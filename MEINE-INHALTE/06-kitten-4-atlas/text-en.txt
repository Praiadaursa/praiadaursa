Farbschlag: Blue Tabby Point
---
Atlas sees the world through one eye — and still doesn't miss a thing! He is always first on the scene when there is something to discover, races his siblings to the top of every climb, and knows exactly when and where dinner is served.

Cats cope remarkably well with one eye, and Atlas is the best proof of it: he plays, romps and cuddles just like his siblings. Whatever he lacks on one side, he more than makes up for with curiosity, courage and an extra-large helping of charm.

Atlas is not looking for a family that will take him anyway — he is looking for one that is looking for exactly him. People with a big heart for the extraordinary, who will love him just as he is. Anyone who has looked him in the eye will understand why.
