Yoselin — expressive, spirited and simply adorable.

She likes to sleep right in the middle of the bed, hides cleverly behind the pillows, and regards a freshly folded pile of laundry as her personal playground.

Honestly: before her, we never quite believed there was much difference between an ordinary house cat and a pedigree cat. But Birmans really are different — utterly devoted to their people, wonderfully talkative, and loyal companions at every step. Yoselin grumbles at us when the food is not to her liking, follows us around the flat like a little dog, and claims your lap the moment you so much as sit down. A cat with character!
