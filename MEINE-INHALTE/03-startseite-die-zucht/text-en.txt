Praia da Ursa — one of the most beautiful coves we have ever set eyes on. Grey cliffs rising steeply out of the water, pale sand, deep blue sea and a swell that simply leaves you in awe. You could almost believe the Sacred Birman copied her markings and her colours straight from this bay!

And yet the truly famous beach lies right next door. Just like this small, hidden cove, we are no large breeding operation: we have only our girl Yoselin, and once a year a litter of little bundles of joy sweeps through our home — like the swell through that bay in Portugal!
