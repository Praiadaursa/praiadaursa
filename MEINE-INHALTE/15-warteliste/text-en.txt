---
We have at most one litter a year and only plan the next one once enough families are waiting for a kitten. If you are dreaming of a little Birman soul, add your name: your request really does help decide when kittens tumble through our home again.

===

Schritt: Write to us
---
On WhatsApp or by email, with no obligation. Tell us a little about yourself and what you are hoping for.

===

Schritt: You are on the waiting list
---
That's you noted down with us, without obligation and free of charge. We get to know each other over the phone, on Zoom or on a visit in Trier, because we want to know where our kittens will live. As soon as planning for the next litter begins, you hear it straight from us.

===

Schritt: Planning begins
---
We have enough families! From here on you are right in the middle of it: you receive every date from us and hear first, as soon as the kittens are here and when they can move in. It only becomes binding with a reservation, and only then is the reservation fee due.

===

Schritt: You are part of the family
---
From the very first day you are part of our family. You receive photos, videos and news from the kitten room, and you have a say in choosing your kitten and its name.

===

Titel: Tell us about yourself
---
The more we know about you, the better we can judge which kitten suits you:

- how you live and whether there is a secured balcony or garden
- which animals already live with you – remember that our kittens only move to a home with a second cat
- whether there are children in the household
- how much time you spend at home
- whether you would like a female or a male and whether you have a favourite colour
- from when a kitten could move in with you

===

Titel: Good to know
---
Our kittens leave at twelve weeks at the earliest, with pedigree, vaccination record, microchip and EU pet passport. What a kitten costs and how rehoming works in detail is explained under [rehoming & price](vermittlung.html). Answers to further questions are in our [FAQ](fragen.html).
