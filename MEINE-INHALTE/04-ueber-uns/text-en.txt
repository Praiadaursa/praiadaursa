Titel: Raised in the middle of life
Bildbeschreibung: Kittens in the living room
---
Our kittens do not grow up behind closed doors — they grow up right in the middle of ordinary everyday chaos! After the first sheltered weeks, it is straight into the thick of things. Our little ones learn early that the vacuum cleaner does not bite, that Willow the dog makes an excellent playmate, and that visitors usually mean treats.

===

Titel: Few litters, plenty of time
---
We do not run a production line. We deliberately allow the mother long breaks and give the youngsters our full attention. That way we know exactly, for every single kitten, who is the cuddler and who is behind the mischief.

===

Titel: Registered with Felidae e.V.
---
Transparency and the wellbeing of our animals come first. We are a registered member of Felidae e.V. and breed strictly according to its breeding and registration rules. For you that means: every one of our kittens moves into its new home with an official, recognised pedigree and the best possible health care.
