Luigi — calm personified and a true gentleman on four paws

When chaos reigns all around him, Luigi simply stays stretched out and watches the spectacle with stoic serenity. Hurry? Not in his vocabulary. He is so good-natured that he would not hurt a fly — not even one cheeky enough to land on his nose.

What he loves above all is company: human or animal, everyone gets a warm welcome, and you have barely sat down before he comes over for a cuddle. This gentle, sociable nature is exactly what we wish for our kittens.
