Cat to go? Not with us. We want people and kitten to genuinely suit one another, and we take our time getting to know you.

===

Titel: Enquiry
---
Simply write to us on WhatsApp or by email and tell us about yourselves.

===

Titel: Getting to know each other
---
If you like, just come and visit us! And if the journey is too far: we do live in the 21st century — a first meeting over Zoom or by phone works just as well.

===

Titel: Reservation
---
Ready for the adventure with a little rocket?

If the chemistry is right and you are sure, we will reserve the kitten for you. The reservation fee is 300 € and goes straight towards the purchase price. From that moment you are right there with us: we keep you supplied with fresh updates until moving day.

===

Titel: Moving out
---
The move happens at twelve weeks of age at the earliest. Your little one arrives with everything needed for a perfect start: the official pedigree, vaccination record including chip number, EU pet passport, the food they are used to for the first few days, and a blanket carrying the familiar scent of mum and siblings.

A special extra: full insurance cover is included up to and including the fourteenth week of life. After that you simply decide for yourselves whether to continue it.

===

Titel: Afterwards
---
We will not leave you out in the rain. Even after the move you can come to us with any question at any time. And should life ever make it impossible to keep the cat: she always comes back to us first. Animal shelters are absolutely out of the question.
