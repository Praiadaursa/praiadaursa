Farbschlag: Black Silver Tabby Point
---
From the smallest of the litter to the undisputed boss of the gang! Ash keeps his siblings thoroughly on their toes, and the moment there is so much as a hint of milk in the air, there is no stopping him.

Besides his wonderful character, he is a real eye-catcher: as a Black Silver Tabby Point his coat gleams in a breathtaking silver-grey. This little rascal is urgently looking for a playmate who can keep up!

This little rocket definitely needs someone with just as much appetite for romping and playing as he has.
