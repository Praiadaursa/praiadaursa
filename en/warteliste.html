Waiting list for Birman kittens The waiting list

We have at most one litter a year, and we only plan the next one once enough people are genuinely waiting for a kitten. So your place on the waiting list really does help decide when kittens tumble through our home again.

How it works

  1. Write to us

    On WhatsApp or by email, with no obligation. Tell us a little about yourself and what you are hoping for.

  2. We get to know each other

    On the phone, over Zoom or during a visit to Trier. We want to know where our kittens will live – and you should know where they come from.

  3. You are on the list

    That reserves your place. Nothing becomes binding until a reservation, and only then is the reservation fee due.

  4. We get in touch

    As soon as a litter is planned and as soon as the kittens arrive, you will be among the first to hear – with photos, we promise.

What to tell us

The more we know about you, the better we can judge which kitten suits you:

  • how you live and whether there is a secured balcony or garden
  • which animals already live with you – remember that our kittens only move to a home with a second cat
  • whether there are children in the household
  • how much time you spend at home
  • whether you would like a female or a male and whether you have a favourite colour
  • from when a kitten could move in with you

Good to know

Our kittens leave at twelve weeks at the earliest, with pedigree, vaccination record, microchip and EU pet passport. What a kitten costs and how rehoming works in detail is explained under rehoming & price. Answers to further questions are in our FAQ.
