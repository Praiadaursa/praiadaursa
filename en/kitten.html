Sacred Birman kittens A litter

Four little rascals have taken over our living room. They are growing up in the middle of family life, accompanied by plenty of love, security and care. Everyday noises and other animals are nothing new to them: our dog Willow is the best playmate there is, and our second cat Hailey looks after the little ones just as affectionately.

The four may move out at twelve weeks at the earliest — by then they are vaccinated, chipped, dewormed, insured, and carry their Felidae e.V. pedigree with them.

Ash

Male · Blue Silver Tabby Point

Found a family

From the smallest of the litter to the undisputed boss of the gang! Ash keeps his siblings thoroughly on their toes, and the moment there is so much as a hint of milk in the air, there is no stopping him.

Besides his wonderful character, he is a real eye-catcher: as a Blue Silver Tabby Point his coat gleams in a breathtaking silver-grey. This little rascal is urgently looking for a playmate who can keep up!

This little rocket definitely needs someone with just as much appetite for romping and playing as he has.

Amber

Female · Seal Tabby Point

Available

When all her siblings are already fast asleep, she is still bouncing through the flat like a hare in ecstasy! And it is not only her siblings who serve as playthings, but preferably mum's fluffy tail — perfect for sinking your teeth into, after all. What the little mouse rather likes to forget: mum enjoys the rough and tumble at least as much... and is unfortunately still the stronger one!

Autumn

Female · Seal Tabby Point

Found a family

While her siblings are still puzzling things out, Autumn has already grasped the rules of life. The litter tray was mastered first time, and wet food gets eaten rather than turned into a mud playground. She is even the only one so far to have understood the most important rule of all: being picked up means food — so absolutely no reason to bring the house down.

Atlas

Male · Blue Tabby Point

Found a family

Atlas sees the world through one eye — and still wants to see more of it than anyone else! No box is too deep, no shelf too high and no corner too dark: our little adventurer simply has to have been everywhere. And when he returns from his expeditions, he heads straight for the nearest lap.

Because Atlas is by far the most trusting kitten in the litter: he comes to greet everyone, happily lets himself be picked up and starts purring the moment you touch him. Cats cope remarkably well with one eye — and Atlas is the best proof of it.

Atlas is not looking for a family that will take him anyway — he is looking for one that is looking for exactly him: people with a big heart for the extraordinary, who love his explorer's spirit and take him just as he is. Anyone who has looked him in the eye will understand why.
